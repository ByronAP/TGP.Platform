# Explicit Brand Style and Compliance Guide (V1.1)

This guide serves as the team's "source of truth," ensuring brand **consistency** across all mediums (UI, pitch decks, marketing materials).

## Section 1: Brand Philosophy and Verbal Identity

The foundation of the brand must prioritize the audience's deep concern for safety and security.

| Element | Specification | Rationale (Citations) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Values** | **Trust, Reliability, and Transparency** | These values must be at the heart of the brand identity to instill trust in customers, as credibility is key in the online safety market. |
| **Messaging Focus** | **Connection over Control** | The brand must be positioned as a **conversation starter**. Control-based strategies like "spying" or setting up "gotcha" moments can **backfire** as children get older and should be avoided. |
| **Tone & Voice** | **Reassuring and Supportive** | Communications should **assure your kids that you are not a spy**. The purpose of the tools is to help **verify** that everyone is making good choices, recognizing that only parents can *teach* these skills. |
| **Consistency** | **Standardized Content** | Define the USP and tagline now. Standardizing communication makes key messaging "copy and paste" for efficiency across the team, which is vital as the company grows. |

## Section 3: Critical Compliance and Design Requirements

If the service is directed toward children under 13 and collects personal information (which includes **persistent identifiers, photos, video, audio, or geolocation** sufficient to identify a street name), the following design standards are mandatory under COPPA. Violations can result in civil penalties up to **$53,088 per violation**.

### 3.1 Privacy Policy Placement and Prominence

Front-end designers must integrate the privacy policy link into the core UI flow according to federal requirements.

| Requirement | Explicit Design Specification | Rationale (Citations) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mandatory Placement** | A clearly labeled link must be posted on the **home or landing page or screen** of the website or online service. | This ensures visitors can easily learn about the operator’s information practices. |
| **Collection Point Placement** | The link must also be present at **each area** of the site or service where personal information is collected from children. | The link must be in **close proximity** to the requests for information in each such area. |
| **Prominence Rule** | The link **must stand out and be noticeable**. It must be visually distinct from other links. | The Commission does **not** consider a link in **small print at the bottom of the home page** to be clear and prominent. |
| **Sizing and Contrast** | Use a **larger font size**, a **different color** (e.g., Accent Orange: **#FF9800**), or a **contrasting background** to fulfill the "clear and prominent" standard. | This is required to ensure the notice functions as an effective "just-in-time" message to parents. |

### 3.2 Direct Notice Requirement

The UI/UX must incorporate a notice step *before* any personal information is collected from a child.

*   **Rule:** The operator must provide **direct notice to parents** and obtain verifiable parental consent **before** collecting personal information.
*   **Design Flow:** The direct notice page or pop-up **must contain certain key information within the four corners of the notice itself**. It is **not sufficient** to send a simple email containing only a link to the policy.

### 3.3 Data Minimization (Spacing and Scope)

*   **Data Collection:** Designers must ensure the app **does not condition a child’s participation** in an activity on the child providing **more information than is reasonably necessary** for that activity.
*   **General Spacing:** Maintain a professional and minimalist layout. Avoid clutter. While numerical spacing isn't explicitly defined, the principle is that visual **balance** and clarity are key to instilling trust.

## Section 4: Maintenance and External Communication

A professional style guide **greases the wheels in the right direction** and makes it **easier to sign up new customers and builds trust**.

*   **Design Review:** Since early guides should be **simple and loose** to allow flexibility, schedule a **review and rebrand benchmark** (e.g., every two years) to ensure the branding evolves successfully as the company scales.
*   **External Partners:** This guide must be used when working with freelancers, agencies, or **partner operating material** to ensure **consistency** in imagery, fonts, and tone.
*   **Building Trust:** Implement brand strategy by emphasizing **transparency** and engaging in **community engagement** and with **parenting influencers**, as peer recommendations carry more weight than traditional ads.
